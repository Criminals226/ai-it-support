# Email Troubleshooting

## Problem
The user cannot send, receive, or access organizational email.

## Troubleshooting Steps

1. Check whether the device has an internet connection.
2. Confirm that the organization's email service is available.
3. Restart the email application.
4. Check whether the mailbox is full.
5. Confirm that the user is signed into the correct organizational account.
6. Check whether the issue occurs in the organization's approved webmail interface.
7. Restart the device if appropriate.
8. If the problem continues, contact IT support.

## Security Notes

- Never request the user's email password.
- Do not open suspicious email attachments or links during troubleshooting.
- Report suspected phishing emails to the organization's security or IT team.
- Do not forward confidential organizational information to personal email accounts.

## Escalation

Create an IT support ticket if:

- The user cannot access email after troubleshooting.
- Multiple users cannot access email.
- The email service appears unavailable.
- The user reports suspicious or potentially malicious email activity.
